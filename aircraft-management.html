The standard

A managed aircraft should never surprise its owner.

Not with a due item that appeared from nowhere, not with a quote that doubled, not with a records gap discovered at the pre-buy. Management is the discipline of knowing before you need to.

We are founder-operated and will stay that way for the aircraft we manage: the A&P who scoped your program is the one on the phone, under the panel and signing the logbook.

How it runs

From first call to steady state.

Questions

What owners ask first.

Anything not covered, call the hotline or send the form.

What does management cost?

An annual retainer scaled by aircraft type, with all labor we perform at the managed rate. It is quoted per aircraft after the records baseline — we do not price an aircraft we have not read.

Can you take over mid-program?

Yes. Most managed aircraft arrive mid-cycle. The baseline reconciles the due list against the records and we take it from there; nothing restarts.

Where is the work done?

At your home base, by us, for anything within our scope. Heavy and specialised work is placed with the right facility and overseen on site. We are mobile and based at Savannah (KSAV).

What if the aircraft is not in the Southeast?

We travel. Routine inputs are planned around it; AoG is mobilised from wherever we are. Travel is itemised, never buried in labor.

Who holds the records?

You do. They live on the OnChain Aviation tenant of IronFleet aviOS under your ownership; we maintain them. If the relationship ends, the record goes with the aircraft, complete.

Do you manage non-Gulfstream aircraft?

No. GIV through G800, including the G280 family. Mixed fleets are scoped per Gulfstream tail.
