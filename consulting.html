How is consulting priced?

Fixed fee per engagement, agreed in writing after a scoping call. Operations reviews and records plans are typically one to three weeks of work; AI implementation is scoped against your systems.

Do you only advise Gulfstream operators?

Maintenance work is Gulfstream only. Operations and AI advisory is shaped by Gulfstream experience but applies to any Part 91 or 135 department that runs a due list and a records room.

Will you recommend IronFleet?

Where it fits. We built on it and run on it, and we say so. Where your existing tracking system is doing the job, the recommendation is to keep it and fix the process around it.
