General

The basics.

Which Gulfstream models do you work on?

GIV through G800, including the G280 family. Twenty-five years in aviation maintenance, twenty of them on Gulfstream — airframe, powerplant and systems.

Are you mobile? Where do you respond?

Yes. We are a mobile operation based at Savannah (KSAV). On-site service at your home base or your preferred facility; AoG response travels wherever the aircraft is.

Do you work with management companies and Part 135 operators?

Yes — Part 91 flight departments, Part 135 operators and management companies with Gulfstream aircraft in the fleet. Fleet arrangements are scoped per aircraft.

What are your credentials?

FAA Airframe & Powerplant. USMC veteran; Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB). NBAA member. Part 145 repair-station application in progress.

Aircraft Management · Tier 01

The retainer.

What does aircraft management include?

One A&P accountable for the maintenance program, the records, the vendors and the schedule. Calendar, hourly and light scheduled events planned and tracked; AD compliance and due lists kept current in IronFleet aviOS; sourcing and sign-off on any work sent out; priority AoG and preferred rates.

What does management cost?

An annual retainer scaled by aircraft type, with all labor we perform at the managed rate. It is quoted per aircraft after the records baseline — we do not price an aircraft we have not read.

Can you take over mid-program?

Yes. Most managed aircraft arrive mid-cycle. The baseline reconciles the due list against the records and we take it from there; nothing restarts.

Where is the work done?

At your home base, by us, for anything within our scope. Heavy and specialised work is placed with the right facility and overseen on site. We are mobile and based at Savannah (KSAV).

Who holds the records?

You do. They live on the OnChain Aviation tenant of IronFleet aviOS under your ownership; we maintain them. If the relationship ends, the record goes with the aircraft, complete.

Do you manage non-Gulfstream aircraft?

No. GIV through G800, including the G280 family. Mixed fleets are scoped per Gulfstream tail.

Technical Services · Tier 02

Pre-buy, records, delivery.

Do you do pre-purchase inspections?

We represent the buyer through the PPI — records and logbook audit, AD and compliance review, oversight of the inspecting facility, and a written report from LOI to closing. It is often the first job before a management agreement.

Can you oversee a PPI at a facility I did not choose?

Yes. Most pre-buys happen at a facility chosen by the seller or the broker. We work with the facility on scope and findings and report to you — our only client in the transaction is the buyer.

What do you need to start a records audit?

Tail or serial number, the current status report if one exists, and access to the logbooks and work-order files — paper, scans or a records platform. We tell you within a day whether the set is complete enough to audit.

Does a technical-services job commit me to a management agreement?

No. Each event stands alone. Many owners start with a pre-buy or a records audit and move to management later; plenty do not.

How is it priced?

Per event, in writing, before we mobilise. Owner’s representation and oversight are day-rated; audits, deliveries and surveys are scoped per aircraft. Travel is itemised. Managed aircraft receive the managed rate.

Do you work on anything other than Gulfstream?

No. GIV through G800, including the G280 family. If the aircraft is something else we will say so and point you to someone good.

Is this blockchain?

No. The record lives in IronFleet aviOS. On-chain anchoring stays on the roadmap for when it earns its place — the record is what matters today.

Do I have to use the platform to use the shop?

Fleet

Coverage by family.

Do you work on the G280?

Yes. It is the one super mid-size on the list and runs through the same process and the same record as the large-cabin fleet.

What about Bombardier, Dassault or Embraer?

No. We are Gulfstream only, by design. We will tell you on the first call and point you to someone good.

Which families do you have factory training on?

FlightSafety Savannah maintenance and avionics training on G350/G450, G500/G550, G650 and G500/G600, with engine run & taxi on the G450, G650 and G500/G600. GIV and G700/G800 are covered under the same A&P with type-family experience.

Does coverage include heavy checks?

Calendar and hourly inspections, light scheduled work, troubleshooting and AD compliance are performed by us. Heavy inspections and specialised work are placed with the right facility and overseen by us — see Aircraft Management.
