Core competencies

What we deliver on contract.

The same four capabilities we deliver commercially, documented to a standard a contracting officer can audit.

01

Mobile Gulfstream maintenance

Calendar and hourly inspections, light scheduled maintenance, troubleshooting and AD compliance on GIV through G800 — performed at the aircraft’s location. No ferry.

02

AoG response

24/7 hotline, remote defect isolation, mobile return-to-service for grounded Gulfstream aircraft.

03

Records audits & technical representation

Forensic logbook and AD-status audits, pre-purchase and delivery-acceptance oversight, owner’s or agency technical representation.

04

Site surveys & condition reports

Aircraft, facility and program readiness assessments with written, prioritised findings.

Questions

What contracting officers ask first.

Anything not covered, call the hotline or send the form.

Are you SDVOSB?

We are certified as a Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB). We do not represent ourselves as service-disabled.

Can you work on government-owned or contractor-operated Gulfstream aircraft?

Yes, within the scope of the founder’s A&P and the applicable maintenance program. Heavy or specialised work is coordinated with an appropriately rated facility and overseen by us.

Where can you deploy?

We are mobile from Savannah, GA. Continental U.S. deployments are planned per task order; travel is itemised.

How do I get the capability statement?

Email mike@onchain.aero with the agency or prime and the requirement. CAGE, UEI, PSC codes and references are included.
