Provenance

A logbook that leaves with the aircraft.

Maintenance records decide what an aircraft is worth. Ours are written once, kept current, and readable by whoever you authorise — at sale, at audit, at the next input.

  1. 01

    The work is performed

    Inspection, repair or scheduled task completed and signed off exactly as it always has been.

  2. 02

    The record is captured

    The entry, findings and sign-off are recorded in aviOS against the aircraft — not left as a PDF in someone’s inbox.

  3. 03

    Anyone you authorise can read it

    A buyer, an insurer or a DOM sees the same history you do, in the order it happened — without calling us.

Who sees what

Your record. Your access list.

The owner authorises; everyone else reads what they are authorised to read, in the order it happened.

  1. 01

    Owner and flight department

    Everything — due list, discrepancies, work orders, documents, the sign-off trail. Operator access is provisioned per managed aircraft.

  2. 02

    Buyer’s representative

    Read access for the pre-buy window: logbooks, AD status, LLP traceability, repairs and alterations. Revoked at closing or transferred with the aircraft.

  3. 03

    Insurer, lender, auditor

    The same history, scoped to what they need to see, without a call to us and without a PDF export that goes stale the day it is sent.

  4. 04

    The next owner

    The record belongs to the aircraft. Change shops, change owners — the history stays with the tail number, complete and readable.

About the platform.

What operators ask before they put an aircraft on it.

All questions →

Is this blockchain?

No. The record lives in IronFleet aviOS. On-chain anchoring stays on the roadmap for when it earns its place — the record is what matters today.

Who holds the records?

You do. OnChain Aviation maintains them on your behalf in aviOS; access is provisioned per aircraft and revocable. If you leave, the record goes with the aircraft.

Do I have to use the platform to use the shop?

No. Per-event technical work is documented the conventional way as well. Managed aircraft run on aviOS because that is how one A&P keeps a due list honest.
