Privacy Policy

OnChain Aviation LLC (“OnChain”, “we”), 309 N Rogers St, Pooler, GA 31322. Effective 30 August 2026.

This policy covers the internal QuickBooks Online connector named OnChain Aviation MCP (the “App”). The App is not a public consumer product. It is used by OnChain staff and authorized agents to read company books.

Data we access

With your QuickBooks Online authorization, the App may read accounting data such as company information, accounts, invoices, bills, payments, reports, and related records. We do not sell this data.

How we use it

Data is used only to operate OnChain Aviation’s finance function: cash, spend, runway, reporting, and related decisions. Access is limited to authorized operators of the App.

Sharing

QuickBooks data stays with OnChain except as required by law, or with service providers we use to run the App (for example Intuit/QuickBooks). We do not share it with advertisers.

Retention and security

Tokens and cached records are kept only as long as needed to run the App and are stored with access controls. You may disconnect the App in QuickBooks or Intuit Developer settings.
