01

Pre-purchase inspection oversight & owner’s representation

A pre-buy is a records audit with an airframe attached. We review the logbooks and AD status before the aircraft moves, set the inspection scope with the facility, stand beside the inspection, and separate airworthiness findings from negotiating points — from letter of intent to closing.

For buyers, brokers, buyer’s counsel and family offices acquiring a Gulfstream.

What it covers

  • Records-first review logbooks, AD status, LLP and inspection history before the PPI starts
  • Scope & facility inspection scope set with the facility; quotes and findings reviewed line by line
  • On-site oversight present for the inspection, borescope and functional checks
  • Discrepancy register airworthiness items vs. negotiable items, with cost context
  • Closing support return-to-service documentation and open items tracked to delivery

02

Forensic records & logbook audit

What an aircraft is worth is decided by its records. We reconstruct the maintenance history from the logbooks, work orders, 8130s and AD compliance — and tell you plainly what is complete, what is missing, and what it will cost to make right.

For buyers, sellers preparing to list, insurers and owners who inherited a paper trail.

What it covers

  • Logbook reconciliation airframe, engine and APU books against work orders and 8130s
  • AD & inspection status compliance verified item by item, not taken from the last summary
  • Gaps & exposure missing entries, unsupported parts, open items — ranked by consequence
  • Digital archive the reviewed record captured in IronFleet aviOS, searchable from day one

How we think about it

The airframe tells you what the aircraft is today. The logbooks tell you what it will cost.

Most pre-buys start with the inspection and get to the records last. We run it the other way round: the paper first, because that is where the surprises live and where the money changes hands.

Every technical-services engagement ends the same way — a written deliverable you can hand to counsel, an insurer or the next owner, and a record of the event captured in IronFleet aviOS against the tail number.

Questions

What buyers ask first.

Anything not covered, call the hotline or send the form.

Can you oversee a PPI at a facility I did not choose?

Yes. Most pre-buys happen at a facility chosen by the seller or the broker. We work with the facility on scope and findings and report to you — our only client in the transaction is the buyer.

What do you need to start a records audit?

Tail or serial number, the current status report if one exists, and access to the logbooks and work-order files — paper, scans or a records platform. We tell you within a day whether the set is complete enough to audit.

Do you work on anything other than Gulfstream?

No. GIV through G800, including the G280 family. If the aircraft is something else we will say so and point you to someone good.

Does a technical-services job commit me to a management agreement?

No. Each event stands alone. Many owners start with a pre-buy or a records audit and move to management later; plenty do not.

How is it priced?

Per event, in writing, before we mobilise. Owner’s representation and oversight are day-rated; audits, deliveries and surveys are scoped per aircraft. Travel is itemised. Managed aircraft receive the managed rate.
